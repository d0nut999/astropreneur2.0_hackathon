# Fictional Company — Employee Handbook (demo)

All content is fictional and written for the HireGuard demo.

## 1. Annual paid leave
Every employee has 30 days of paid annual leave per calendar year (1 January to 31 December). Only working days (Monday to Friday) are counted. Unused days cannot be carried over to the next year, except with written approval from HR. Leave requests should be sent to HR at least 5 working days before the first day of leave.

## 2. How leave requests are decided
HR decides every leave request. A request is normally accepted when the employee still has enough days and at least 70% of their team remains at work on each day. If a team would fall below 70%, HR may propose other dates. An employee who has used all 30 days can ask for a meeting with HR to explain an exceptional situation.

## 3. Sick leave
An employee who is sick informs HR on the first day of absence and sends a medical certificate within 48 hours. Sick leave is never refused for staffing reasons. The company does not ask for or store any diagnosis. After 60 days of sick leave in one calendar year, HR contacts the employee to discuss support, occupational health and a possible adjusted return to work.

## 4. Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week, agreed with their manager. Remote days cannot be taken on the team's weekly meeting day. Equipment for home office (laptop, headset) is provided by the company.

## 5. Working hours
The standard working week is 40 hours, Monday to Friday, with core hours from 10:00 to 16:00. Lunch break is 1 hour between 12:00 and 14:00. Overtime must be approved in advance by the manager and is compensated with time off.

## 6. Expenses
Business travel and work expenses are reimbursed within 30 days. Receipts must be submitted through the expense form within one month of the expense. Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to 25 EUR per day.

## 7. Benefits
Employees receive health insurance from the first day, a yearly training budget of 500 EUR, and a transport allowance. Requests to use the training budget are sent to HR with the course name and price.

## 8. Parental leave
Mothers receive 14 weeks of paid maternity leave and fathers or second parents 10 working days of paid leave, taken within 4 months of the birth or adoption. Parental leave does not reduce the annual leave allowance.

## 9. Contact HR
For any question not covered in this handbook, write to hr@fictional-company.test. HR answers within 2 working days.
